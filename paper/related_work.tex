\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Hallucination detection in LLMs.}
The faithfulness of autoregressive LLM outputs has attracted significant attention in high-stakes deployments \citep{huang2023survey, ji2023survey}. Approaches span confidence-based signals, internal-state probes, and external grounding: \citet{farquhar2024semantic} cluster semantically equivalent outputs (Semantic Entropy), later approximated cheaply from single-generation hidden states \citep{kossen2024semantic}; \citet{manakul2023selfcheckgpt} (SelfCheckGPT) use black-box sample consistency; \citet{kadavath2022know} train models to predict their own P(IK); \citet{burns2022discovering,marks2023geometry} find hidden-state directions tracking truth independent of stated output; \citet{chen2024inside} (INSIDE) use response-covariance eigenspectra; \citet{orgad2024llms} show truthfulness concentrates in specific answer tokens but generalizes poorly across datasets -- a caution we return to; \citet{azaria2023internal,chuang2024lookback} show hidden-state linear probes directly encode correctness. We audit whether \emph{attention-graph} topological features add signal beyond these simpler internal probes, finding the $0$D component is exactly a Minimum Spanning Tree statistic (Theorem~\ref{thm:mst}) computable at a fraction of the cost -- and, in most but not all of our audited settings, strongly correlated with (though not reducible to, \S\ref{sec:method}) simple non-topological attention statistics.

\paragraph{Benchmark artifacts.} Concurrent with our audit, \citet{hussain2026parallax} (PARALLAX) show several hallucination corpora built like HaluEval (correct/incorrect answer pairs alongside the prompt) admit near-perfect detection from surface text alone, and \citet{janiak2025illusion} independently show response-length heuristics can rival sophisticated detectors. We confirm this directly for HaluEval QA (\S\ref{sec:lexical-confound}): AUC $0.96$--$0.98$ from answer length or bag-of-words alone, surviving standard length matching -- a confound independent of the sequence-length effect we identify for $1$D persistence (Proposition~\ref{prop:scaling}), affecting any probe on this benchmark.

\paragraph{Topological data analysis in NLP.} Persistent homology on NLP representations was pioneered by \citet{kushnareva2021,cherniavskii2022} (BERT attention barcodes vs.\ syntactic acceptability) and extended to uncertainty estimation \citep{kostenok2023} and human-vs-machine text separation via persistence-dimension \citep{tulchinskii2023intrinsic}; \citet{barannikov2022rtd} introduce Representation Topology Divergence, and \citet{magai2022topology} survey topological tools for deep representations. TOHA \citep{toha} computes a topological divergence statistic (MTop-Div) for hallucination detection; CHARM \citep{charm} generalizes attention heuristics into a graph-neural-network framework -- an architecturally different, learned method out of scope for direct reproduction here (\S\ref{sec:audit}). We formally audit the $0$D/$1$D persistent-homology family these build on: Theorem~\ref{thm:mst} proves $0$D features are exactly Kruskal's MST, and TOST shows $1$D cycles add only a small, largely length-confounded increment (\S\ref{sec:audit}).

\paragraph{Mathematical auditing, equivalence testing, and interpretability.} \citet{balestriero2021} show that ReLU networks are secretly max-affine spline operators -- a classical construction in disguise, the same rhetorical move Theorem~\ref{thm:mst} makes for attention-graph TDA; \citet{kapoor2023leakage} document how undetected data leakage inflates reported performance across ML-based science, and \citet{gururangan2018annotation,mccoy2019right} show NLI benchmarks are separable via superficial annotation artifacts and syntactic heuristics rather than the reasoning they are meant to test -- both consistent with our length and lexical confounds on HaluEval QA. TOST \citep{walker2011,lakens2017equivalence} converts underpowered null tests into calibrated, falsifiable equivalence claims, which we adopt throughout. Jet Expansions \citep{chen2026jets} show a widely-used heuristic (Logit Lens) is a special case of a more general residual-stream decomposition -- the same move Theorem~\ref{thm:mst} makes for attention-graph TDA. Standard Vietoris--Rips complexes also discard attention's directionality via symmetrization ($W_{ij}=\max(A_{ij},A_{ji})$); directed flag complexes \citep{grigoryan2018}, path-homology \citep{grigoryan2014}, and zigzag persistence \citep{carlsson2010} could recover what is lost. We restrict our audit to the static symmetrized framework used in TOHA (Appendix~\ref{app:assumptions}); extending it to directed/dynamic filtrations is future work.
